CerebralHelm Canvas Sync

Pairs this extension with your local CerebralHelm app so your School dashboard shows your courses, grades, and upcoming deadlines. Everything stays on your Mac.

  1. In CerebralHelm, open Settings → Setup → Canvas and copy the token.
  2. Paste it above and click Save token.
  3. Click Send test scrape — CerebralHelm's School widgets should fill in.
