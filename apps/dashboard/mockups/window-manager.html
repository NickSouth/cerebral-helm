Window manager. Half the previous width, on a Dock-like slab — translucent, but with enough of its own ground that the cards never get lost in whatever is behind the window. The slab is shaded dark at top and bottom with a lift through the middle, so it reads as a cylinder and agrees with the wheel turning inside it. Apps with several windows show as a stack; click one to fan it out. Scroll to feel the curve.
